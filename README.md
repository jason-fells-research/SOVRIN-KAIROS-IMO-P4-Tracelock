# SOVRIN-KAIROS and IMO 2020 Problem 4

A white paper on reasoning you can trace from the first step to the answer. SOVRIN-KAIROS works through Problem 4 of the 2020 International Mathematical Olympiad, catches and corrects its own error, and keeps a hashed record of every step.

[Read the paper](https://codepen.io/Jason-Fells/full/PwPNxJb/38250055673b4b7a860509a937b5dd1d)

## Scope

- The SOVRIN-KAIROS architecture: the M1 to M13 module stack and its review protocol.
- The case study: IMO 2020 Problem 4, with the error the system found and how it corrected it.
- The record: each reasoning step hashed with SHA-256, so anyone can check it.
- Regulation: how the method maps to the EU AI Act (Articles 17 and 18), ISO/IEC 42001:2023 and NIST AI RMF 1.0.
- A reading view that switches between the paper and its full record.

## Audience

Researchers, regulators, legal and compliance teams, enterprise teams and investors reviewing AI reasoning.
